YOUR CONTENT

Callsigns and gameplay you submit appear publicly on leaderboards. Keep callsigns suitable for everyone; moderated names may be rejected. You retain whatever rights you hold in your submissions and grant us the rights needed to display and verify them here.

SMS ALERTS

ArcadeBench operates a Twilio phone number for operational SMS alerts (experiment status, service health). The only current recipient is the operator, who opts in through his own alerting configuration. No player or visitor phone numbers are collected or texted.

Message frequency varies with alert settings, up to roughly 50 messages per day. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time and HELP for help. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
