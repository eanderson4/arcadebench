An arcade for everyone.

ArcadeBench is a growing, free public collection of browser arcade games, made in the spirit of the early arcade era.

Quick to start, deep enough to chase

The aim is simple: pick a game, learn a few controls, and start playing. The rules stay small enough to hold in your head. The depth arrives while you play: patterns surface in the field, a score creeps toward your best, a run ends one move short and you start again because now you know what to do.

That is the whole idea. Learn by playing, chase the score, take one more try.

The promise

No ads. No microtransactions. No paid advantages. No gacha or loot boxes. Progress comes from learning the game and improving your play. ArcadeBench is free to play and meant to stay that way.

Community supported

ArcadeBench is supported by donations and contributions from the community.

Code, art, ideas, and playtesting all help keep the arcade growing and free to play.
