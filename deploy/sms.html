ArcadeBench operates +1 (541) 406-3448 for operational text alerts. This page describes the program: what the messages are, who gets them, and how to opt in or out.

SHORT ENOUGH TO READ.
SPECIFIC ENOUGH TO HOLD US TO IT.

WHAT THE MESSAGES ARE

Operational alerts about ArcadeBench and the experiments its operator runs: experiment training progress and completion, scheduled-job failures (for example the nightly replay-retention cleanup), and arcadebench.org health-check failures. Messages are factual status notes, never marketing.

WHO RECEIVES THEM

The only recipient is the ArcadeBench operator, Eric Anderson, who uses the alerts to monitor long-running experiments and service uptime. No player, visitor, or customer phone numbers are collected or texted.

HOW TO OPT IN

Text START to +1 (541) 406-3448. You will receive a confirmation message:

ArcadeBench: You are now opted in to operational SMS alerts. Msg frequency varies, up to 50/day. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help or STOP to opt out. arcadebench.org

FREQUENCY, COST, AND OPTING OUT

  • Message frequency varies with alert settings, up to roughly 50 messages per day.
  • Message and data rates may apply, depending on your carrier plan.
  • Reply STOP at any time to opt out; reply HELP for help. Opt-out is honored automatically.
  • Mobile phone numbers are never shared, sold, rented, or used for marketing.

THE FINE PRINT, IN PLAIN SIGHT

Data handling is covered by the Privacy Promise and service terms by the Terms of Play. The code behind the alerts is public in the ArcadeBench repository.
