THE PLAIN DEAL / EFFECTIVE AUGUST 18, 2026

PRIVACY
PROMISE

ArcadeBench is a free, non-commercial, open-source arcade. We are here to make games—not to turn players into a dataset.

  • NO DATA BUSINESSWe do not sell player data, build advertising profiles, or run ads.
  • NO AI TRAININGWe do not use gameplay, replays, prompts, or controllers to train AI.
  • REPLAYS EXPIREEvery complete replay expires after five days and is automatically deleted.
SHORT ENOUGH TO READ.
SPECIFIC ENOUGH TO HOLD US TO IT.

WHAT STAYS ON YOUR DEVICE

Ordinary gameplay and replay inspection run in your browser. Loading a replay file does not upload it. Your downloads and your browser's local preview data remain under your control.

WHEN A REPLAY IS UPLOADED

A complete replay reaches ArcadeBench only when you submit a ranked score for verification or explicitly choose Share for 5 days. The server replays its inputs through the open-source game engine to verify the result.

The full replay is scheduled for deletion five days after upload. We permanently retain only its SHA-256 hash and the public leaderboard summary; a hash identifies the verified bytes but cannot play the game back.

WHAT A LEADERBOARD KEEPS

  • The callsign you submit, score, game and level, difficulty, timing, partition count, game version, and submission date.
  • The replay hash and verification status after the replay itself is deleted.
  • Anonymous game and level votes.

ACCOUNTLESS PLAY AND SAFETY

There are no player accounts. A signed anonymous cookie lasts up to 30 days so voting, one-time score challenges, and rate limits work. ArcadeBench does not write IP addresses into its application database.

To keep public callsigns suitable for everyone, only the proposed callsign—not gameplay, replay data, prompts, or controller code—is sent to Cloudflare Workers AI for one safety classification. Approved names appear on leaderboards. Rejected raw names are not stored; the moderation cache keeps a hash.

INFRASTRUCTURE

Cloudflare delivers the site and may process ordinary network and security metadata needed to operate and protect it. ArcadeBench uses Cloudflare D1 for leaderboard and social records and R2 for temporary replay objects.

OPERATOR SMS ALERTS

The ArcadeBench operator uses a Twilio phone number for personal operational alerts (experiment status, service health). The only recipient is the operator, who opts in by configuring his own number in his own alerting tool. No player or visitor phone numbers are collected.

Mobile phone numbers are never shared, sold, rented, or used for marketing. Message frequency varies with alert settings, up to roughly 50 messages per day. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time and HELP for help; alerts can also be disabled entirely in the alerting tool's configuration.

OPEN CODE, HONEST CHANGES

The games, replay verifier, retention job, and this promise are public in the ArcadeBench repository. If our practices change, we will change the code and this page together—not quietly reinterpret the words.
